Grow Your Relationships Like a Garden

Social Roots is a personal CRM that turns your contacts into a living digital garden. Each relationship is a plant — water it regularly, and watch it thrive. Neglect it, and it wilts.

See How It Works
🌿

Your Contacts, Reimagined

Import contacts from your phone or add them manually. Each person becomes a unique plant in your garden, reflecting how strong your connection is.

💧

Water to Stay Connected

"Watering" a plant means you reached out — a call, a text, coffee. Log interactions in one tap and keep your relationships healthy.

📊

Health at a Glance

See which relationships need attention. Plants change appearance based on how recently you've connected, so you never lose touch with the people who matter.

🔔

Gentle Reminders

Get notified before a plant starts wilting. Customisable reminder frequencies let you set the pace that feels right for each relationship.

📝

Notes & Context

Jot down important details about each person — birthdays, preferences, conversation topics — so every interaction feels personal.

🏖️

Vacation Mode

Going offline? Pause the clock on all your plants so they stay healthy while you recharge. Pick up right where you left off.
